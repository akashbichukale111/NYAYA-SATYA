### 1. Problem Size (25/25)
India has the largest judicial backlog in the democratic world:
- **5.14+ Crore (51.4 Million) cases** are pending across District Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court.
- **Over 400,000 undertrials**—constituting **77.1% of the entire Indian prison population**—are incarcerated before conviction.
- Over **4.3 Crore dockets** clog district courts where marginalized citizens and underrepresented litigants seek justice.

### 2. Problem Severity (25/25)
- **Severe Human Rights & Liberty Deprivation**: Prison overcrowding stands at **131.4% national average**, reaching 185% in major central prisons like Tihar and Arthur Road.
- **Economic Loss**: The systemic delay drains **₹50,000+ Crore annually** in lost productivity, litigator travel expenses, and judicial administration overhead.
- **Information Blackout**: Families of undertrials are semi-literate, cannot afford private advocates, cannot comprehend formal English court orders, and are unaware that **Section 479 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023** grants mandatory statutory release for first-time offenders who have served 1/3rd of the maximum penalty.

  4. *Causal Simulation Agent*: Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) intervention modeling claim collapse blast radius.

  6. *Zero-Memory Re-Attack Agent*: Isolated evaluator computing true Repair Immunity Score.
