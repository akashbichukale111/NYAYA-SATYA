## 1. Launching the OS

Run the unified startup script:
```powershell
cd D:\NYAYA-SATYA\os
.\run_os.ps1
```

`http://127.0.0.1:8000`

## 2. Step-by-Step Demonstration Steps

### Step 1: Observe the Unified OS Shell & Command Center
- **Sidebar**: Notice the permanent left navigation bar displaying all 12 engines organized into 5 logical categories (Evidence & Reasoning, Procedural & Compliance, Case Acceleration, Human Rights & Liberty, Practice Automation).
- **Top Command Bar**: Notice the active case selector showing `State (NCT of Delhi) v. Rajesh Kumar (Tis Hazari)`, the global search bar (`Ctrl+K`), the priority Attention pill (`2 CRITICAL`), and the `HUMAN GATE: PENDING APPROVAL` status badge.
- **Cockpit Dashboard**: Notice the Case Hero banner with real-time stats:
  - Custody Duration: `418 Days`
  - Statutory Bail Threshold: `EXCEEDED (+53d)`
  - Hearing Readiness: `78%`
- **12 Live Cards**: Review the 12 interactive engine cards displaying live, synchronized metrics for this case.

### Step 2: Global Search (`Ctrl + K`)
- Press `Ctrl + K` or click the search box.
- Type `bail` or `undertrial` or `defect`.
- Notice instant search across multi-tenant cases, engines, and pending alerts.
- Press `Escape` to close.

### Step 3: Inspect Module 07 — Undertrial Liberty Sentinel
- In the sidebar or on Card M-07, click **Launch View →**.
- Examine the Section 479 BNSS / 436A CrPC audit:
  - Actual Custody: `418 Days`
  - 1/3rd Statutory Threshold: `365 Days`
  - Overdue Detention: `+53 Days`
- Note the statutory guarantee: First-time undertrial who has undergone 1/3rd maximum sentence **SHALL** be released on bail per Section 479(1) BNSS.
- Click **← Back to OS Cockpit**.

### Step 4: Inspect Module 06 — Evidence Dependency Engine
- In the sidebar or Card M-06, click **Evidence Dependency**.
- Observe the mapped claims and their fragility scores:
  - `C1: Presence at Scene of Crime` (HIGH FRAGILITY)
  - `C3: Chain of Custody Maintained` (CRITICAL FRAGILITY)
- Observe the Single-Point Failure analysis highlighting missing Section 63 BSA electronic evidence certificates.

### Step 5: Inspect Module 05 — Procedural Obligation Engine
- In the sidebar or Card M-05, click **Procedural Obligation**.
- Observe the strict **WHO / WHAT / LOSS / SIGN** obligation `OBL-2024-001`:
  - **WHO**: Chief Metropolitan Magistrate & Special Public Prosecutor
  - **WHAT**: Reversible actions (serve omitted Annexure D, request urgent mentioning)
  - **LOSS**: Irreversible risk range: `INR 0 - 50,000` (Loss of hearing date, 45-day cycle delay)
  - **SIGN**: Click the gold button **"Authorize & Sign Obligation"**.
  - Notice the obligation updates immediately to `AUTHORISED` signed by counsel.

### Step 6: UNWIND Human Legal Gate Signoff
- On the top command bar, click **Sign Authorization**.
- Confirm counsel name: `Adv. Meenakshi Sundaram`.
- Notice the top status badge turns emerald green: `HUMAN GATE: AUTHORIZED`.

### Step 7: System Diagnostics & Health Audit
- In the left sidebar under Administration, click **System Diagnostics**.
- Review the comprehensive system health audit:
  - Non-Adjudication Guarantee: `VERIFIED (0% Prediction)`
  - UNWIND Human Gate: `STRICTLY ENFORCED`
  - Case Isolation: `MULTI-TENANT SAFE`
  - 12/12 Modular Engines Health Table.
