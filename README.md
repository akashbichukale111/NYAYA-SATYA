### 🇮🇳 NYAYA-SETU (न्याय-सेतू): Auto-Bail Corridor & Zero-Surety Freedom Protocol
*(PM Vision: Autonomous Digital Public Infrastructure for Timely Justice & Undertrial Liberation)*

Over **77.1% of inmates in Indian prisons are undertrials**, languishing behind bars not because of guilt, but because they lack money for bail bonds (surety) or cannot afford private advocates to file applications. 

To turn Prime Minister Narendra Modi's vision of humane criminal justice and Section 479 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS 2023) into ground reality, NYAYA-SATYA introduces **NYAYA-SETU**:

#### ⚡ Step 1: 08:00 AM Automated Jail Superintendent & Judicial Notice Generator
- **The Ground Bottleneck Solved**: Section 479(3) BNSS explicitly mandates that the Prison Superintendent *shall* apply to the Court on behalf of any undertrial who completes one-third of the maximum sentence. In practice, manual registry audits rarely happen.
- **The Zero-Click Autonomous Trigger**:
  1. Every morning at **08:00:00 IST**, Liberty Sentinel (Engine 07) runs an autonomous cryptographic sweep across e-Prisons records.
  2. The moment an undertrial (e.g. *Rajesh Kumar, FIR 0482/2023*) reaches 365 days (1/3rd threshold), **the system auto-generates a legally binding Section 479(3) notice**.
  3. The notice is **auto-served simultaneously** to:
     - 🏢 **Jail Superintendent (Central Jail Tihar)** via e-Prisons portal to initiate discharge.
     - ⚖️ **Chief Metropolitan Magistrate (Tis Hazari)** to list the suo-motu discharge on the daily board.
     - 🛡️ **DLSA Member Secretary** to appoint a pro-bono panel advocate for zero-cost release formalities.
  4. **Zero-Bribe, Zero-Delay Guarantee**: No paper application from the prisoner is required. No surety bond is demanded for first-time offenders under the Section 479 proviso.
  5. **Live Verification API**: Testable at `POST https://nyaya-satya.onrender.com/api/citizen/nyaya-setu-auto-trigger`.

#### 📄 Step 2: 'ई-मुचलका' (Jan-Dhan / Aadhaar Linked Zero-Rupee Bail Bond)
- **The Ground Bottleneck Solved**: Over 30% of undertrials granted bail in India remain trapped in jail because they lack property documents or cash for sureties, making them easy prey for exploitative bail touts (दलाल).

  1. **Statutory Anchors**: Codified under Section 479(1) Proviso and Section 480 of BNSS 2023, coupled with the Supreme Court's mandate in *Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI (2022)* requiring unconditional personal bonds for indigent prisoners.

  3. **Jan-Dhan & Aadhaar Integration**: Proves BPL indigence in real time via Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) account linkage (`PMJDY-DEL-984210`) and UIDAI virtual token (`XXXX-XXXX-8375`).

## 💥 The National Problem: India's 5-Crore Case Backlog & Undertrial Crisis

India’s judicial system is facing an unprecedented backlog crisis:
- **5.1+ Crore (51 Million) cases** are pending across District Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court.
- **Over 4.3 Crore cases** are bottlenecked at District & Subordinate courts where common citizens seek justice.
- **77.1% of all Indian prison inmates** are undertrial detainees who have not been convicted of any crime.
- **Average case lifespan** extends between **4 to 12 years**, primarily due to non-merit adjournments.

### Why Do Cases Actually Get Stuck? (The Root Causes NYAYA-SATYA Solves)
1. **The "Adjournment Culture" (Tareekh-pe-Tareekh)**: 68% of hearings are adjourned without hearing substantive arguments because of procedural defects: missing witness summons, unfiled rejoinders, or unserved notices.
2. **Fragile Evidentiary Foundations**: Lawyers build arguments on assumptions. In the courtroom, when a single document’s chain of custody fails or a date contradiction is exposed, the trial collapses into an immediate 6-month postponement.
3. **Legal Aid Turnover & Context Loss**: Undertrials rotate through multiple Legal Aid (DLSA) advocates. Each new lawyer receives a cardboard file of hundreds of disorganized papers, taking months just to understand the basic facts.
4. **Registry Defects & Scrutiny Delays**: Cases sit in court registries for weeks because of trivial clerical, formatting, or jurisdiction tagging defects.
5. **The Citizen Information Blackout**: Poor families have zero visibility into their case. They do not know when the next hearing is, what happened in court, or that their family member is legally entitled to statutory bail under the new criminal laws.

## 🚀 Quick Start & Installation

### Prerequisites
- Python 3.12 or 3.13
- Git

### 1. Clone the Repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/akashbichukale111/NYAYA-SATYA.git
cd NYAYA-SATYA
```

### 2. Configure Virtual Environment
```bash
python -m venv .venv
# On Windows:
.venv\Scripts\activate
# On Linux/macOS:
source .venv/bin/activate
```

### 3. Install Dependencies
```bash
pip install -e .
pip install pytest httpx uvicorn
```

### 4. Launch the Gateway Server
```bash
# Launch unified gateway on port 8000
python -m uvicorn os.gateway.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000 --reload
```
