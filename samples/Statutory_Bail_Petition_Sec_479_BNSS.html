MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

  1. That the Applicant is an undertrial prisoner, arrested on 16-March-2023 in connection with FIR No. 0482/2023 registered at P.S. Kashmere Gate under Sections 406/420/120B IPC.
  2. That the Applicant has continuously remained in judicial custody for 428 Days as of 18-October-2024.
  3. STATUTORY ELIGIBILITY UNDER SECTION 479 BNSS:
    Under the primary substantive charge under Section 406 IPC (Criminal Breach of Trust), the maximum sentence is 3 years (1,095 days). Under Section 479(1) First Proviso of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023:
    "Provided that where such person is a first-time offender (who has not previously been convicted of any offence), he shall be released on bail by the Court if he has undergone detention for the period extending up to one-third of the maximum period of imprisonment specified for such offence under that law."
    One-third of 1,095 days is 365 days. The Applicant has already undergone 428 days in custody, therefore surpassing the statutory one-third requirement by 63 days.
  4. That the Applicant has no prior criminal antecedents and is a first-time undertrial as verified by the National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG) and Delhi Police Crime Records.
  5. That this statutory entitlement is mandatory and non-discretionary as held by the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in In Re: Policy Strategy for Grant of Bail (SMWP (Crl.) No. 4/2021).
